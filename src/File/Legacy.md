---
heading: Our Heritage – A Kalpetta Homestay with a Story
---
Kudajadri Drizzle is more than a homestay in Kalpetta; it is a piece of Wayanad’s history. Originally a 100-year-old Jain Tharavadu, the homestay preserves its cultural charm while offering modern amenities. Guests can feel the warmth of traditional architecture, wooden interiors, and heritage décor that reflect the local culture. Staying at one of our Kalpetta homestays provides a rare opportunity to experience Wayanad’s history firsthand.
