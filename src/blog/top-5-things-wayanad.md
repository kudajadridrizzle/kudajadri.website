# Top 5 Things to Do in Wayanad

Wayanad, known as the "Green Paradise of Kerala," offers a perfect blend of adventure, nature, and cultural experiences. Here are the top 5 activities you shouldn't miss during your stay at Kudajadri Heritage Stay.

## 1. Trek to Chembra Peak

**Duration**: 4-5 hours  
**Difficulty**: Moderate to Challenging

Chembra Peak, standing at 2,100 meters above sea level, is the highest peak in Wayanad. The trek offers breathtaking views of the surrounding valleys and the famous heart-shaped lake.

### What to Expect:
- Stunning panoramic views
- Heart-shaped natural lake
- Rich biodiversity
- Cool mountain breeze

## 2. Explore Edakkal Caves

**Duration**: 2-3 hours  
**Difficulty**: Easy to Moderate

These ancient caves feature prehistoric petroglyphs dating back to 6000 BC. The climb to the caves is rewarding with magnificent views of the surrounding landscape.

### Highlights:
- Ancient rock carvings
- Historical significance
- Scenic mountain views
- Archaeological importance

## 3. Visit Soochipara Falls

**Duration**: Half day  
**Difficulty**: Easy

Also known as Sentinel Rock Waterfalls, this three-tiered cascade is perfect for swimming and relaxation. The falls are surrounded by lush green forests.

### Activities:
- Swimming in natural pools
- Rock climbing
- Photography
- Nature walks

## 4. Wildlife Safari at Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary

**Duration**: 3-4 hours  
**Best Time**: Early morning or late afternoon

Home to elephants, tigers, leopards, and various bird species, this sanctuary offers an excellent wildlife viewing experience.

### Wildlife to Spot:
- Asian elephants
- Bengal tigers
- Spotted deer
- Various bird species
- Butterflies

## 5. Spice Plantation Tours

**Duration**: 2-3 hours  
**Best Time**: Morning

Learn about the cultivation of cardamom, pepper, coffee, and other spices that make Kerala famous worldwide.

### Experience:
- Guided plantation walks
- Spice processing demonstrations
- Fresh spice purchases
- Traditional breakfast

## Planning Your Trip

Stay at Kudajadri Heritage Stay as your base to explore all these wonderful attractions. Our location provides easy access to all major tourist spots while offering a peaceful retreat after your adventures.
