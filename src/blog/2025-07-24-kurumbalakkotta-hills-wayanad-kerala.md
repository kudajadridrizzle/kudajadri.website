## Major Trekking-points in Wayanad, Kerala

Wayanad has numerous trekking locations that offer varied trekking trips to mountain tops to catch a glimpse of the breath-taking views from up there. Unending trips to the interiors of this luscious greenery exploring the unseen and the hidden beauty that will soothe your mind as well as fill your lungs with the freshness of purity from the atmosphere.\
\
One of the major popular trekking locations is the Chembra Peak which is a 6900 ft trekking and one among the popular choices of the amateur trekkers. The route is quite straight and steep which will let the traveller explore the unprecedented beauty of the amazing flora and fauna. The heart-shaped lake atop the mountain is a common spot for photographers. An ideal time to explore the Chembra peak is from September to February.\
\
To satisfy the wanderlust in you, the Pakshipathalam trekking is one of the challenging locations to venture trekking. It is at a height of 1740 feet above sea level. The trekking path lets you traverse through the dense forest, thick rocky blocks and caves which is an easy terrain to climb up.\
\
Kalladi forests which are the oldest rain-forests is also breathtaking trek to venture on your visit to Wayanad. The trail is through the beguiling tea gardens that take you through winding roads enveloped by the tea gardens on either side and a river. Once you reach atop the mountain, the misty layer is quite enthralling to witness.\
\
As the bewitching beauty of the Wayanad, there are secrets that are hidden in the outer world.\
\
Kurumbalakotta, a tiny hamlet which is hidden in the lofty rims of Wayanad. The best part of Kurumbalakotta is the spiritually enhancing walk to the abode which is worth taking as the view from up above the mountain is awe-struck.
