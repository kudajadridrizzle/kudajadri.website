# Welcome to Kudajadri Heritage Home

Welcome to Kudajadri, where tradition meets tranquility in the heart of Wayanad's breathtaking landscapes. Our heritage home offers a unique blend of authentic Kerala architecture and modern comfort, providing guests with an unforgettable experience.

## A Legacy of Hospitality

Nestled amidst the rolling hills and lush greenery of Wayanad, Kudajadri has been a symbol of warm hospitality for generations. Our carefully preserved heritage structure tells the story of Kerala's rich cultural heritage while offering all the modern amenities you need for a comfortable stay.

### What Makes Us Special

- **Authentic Heritage Architecture**: Experience the charm of traditional Kerala design
- **Pristine Natural Setting**: Surrounded by coffee plantations and spice gardens
- **Modern Amenities**: All the comfort of contemporary hospitality
- **Local Experiences**: Immerse yourself in the culture of Wayanad

## Explore Wayanad

Wayanad offers countless opportunities for adventure and relaxation:

1. **Chembra Peak**: Trek to the heart-shaped lake
2. **Banasura Sagar Dam**: India's largest earthen dam
3. **Edakkal Caves**: Ancient petroglyphs and stunning views
4. **Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary**: Spot elephants and other wildlife
